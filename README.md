# SJ Computer Education website

## Publish it (beginner steps)

1. Open the `sj-computer-education` folder. It contains `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js`.
2. Sign in or create an account at [Netlify](https://app.netlify.com/).
3. Open [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) and drag the whole `sj-computer-education` folder into the upload area. Netlify will publish a temporary `…netlify.app` address.
4. Open that address on your phone and computer. Test the call links and send a sample enquiry to verify the WhatsApp handoff.
5. After buying `sjcomputereducation.com`, in your Netlify site choose **Domain management → Add a domain → Add a domain you already own**. Add `www.sjcomputereducation.com` and follow the DNS steps Netlify shows for your site. Set the `www` address as primary if Netlify asks.
6. At the domain registrar, add the DNS records Netlify specifies (or follow its option to use Netlify DNS). Add/confirm both `www.sjcomputereducation.com` and `sjcomputereducation.com` in Netlify so the shorter address can redirect to the `www` address.
7. Wait for DNS to update, then open `https://www.sjcomputereducation.com` in your browser. Netlify will provision HTTPS after the domain is connected.

Netlify’s official guide explains manual folder uploads and its custom-domain/DNS steps. DNS changes can take time to reach everyone.

## Domain purchase

The requested domain is `sjcomputereducation.com`. The Verisign .com RDAP endpoint returned **404 / no domain record** in a direct lookup on 31 October 2026. This suggests it may be unregistered, but it is not a reservation or a guarantee of sale: a registrar’s final checkout decides whether it can be registered and at what price. Search the exact name at a registrar such as [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/) or [GoDaddy](https://www.godaddy.com/domains), compare renewal cost as well as first-year price, then register it in the institute owner’s name/email and turn on auto-renew. Buy the domain only; hosting can stay on Netlify.

The form creates a WhatsApp message addressed to `88389 88167`; it does not store submissions or send them to an email inbox. For a working enquiry, the visitor must have WhatsApp available and tap **Send** in the opened chat. If you prefer enquiries by email or a stored form inbox, connect a form service before publishing.

PGDIT appears in the brochure cover; visitors are asked to contact the institute for its outline.
